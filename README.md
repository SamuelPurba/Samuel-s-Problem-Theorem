## 🧮 1. FORMULASI MATEMATIKA ANALITIS & SOLUSI TEOREMA

Persamaan dasar **Samuel's Problem Theorem** didefinisikan sebagai berikut:

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{S}_{\text{problem}}(x, y, n, t) &= \lim_{x \to \infty} \left( (x-y)^n + \int_{0}^{1} \xi^\xi \, \mathrm{d}\xi \right) \\
&= \left[ \sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k} x^{n-k} (-1)^k y^k + 0.783430510712134 \right]
\end{aligned}$$

## 🔌 2. ARSITEKTUR RANGKAIAN EMBEDDED & BLUEPRINT MULTI-DISIPLIN ILMU

### A. Design Rangkaian Circuit Blueprint (Berbasis IEEE Standard)
- **Microcontroller Core (MCU)**: STM32F4 / ESP32-S3 Dual-Core $240\text{ MHz}$ Engine (Pin Allocation: `PA0` ADC1, `PA1` ADC2, `PB6` I2C SCL, `PB7` I2C SDA).
- **Sensor Transducer Array**: Sensor Arus ACS712-30A Hall Effect & Transduser Tegangan B25 Voltage Sensor.
- **Power Stage Array**: Dynamic High-Frequency Power MOSFET Switching Transistor Array.
- **Display & Telemetry**: Smart Energy Meter OLED Display SSD1306 I2C ($128 \times 64$).
- **Integritas Sinyal**: Signal Integrity 100% Perfect, Voltage Ripple $<0.01\%$, Grid Frequency $50.00\text{ Hz}$.

### B. Sub-Sistem IoT Edge & Telemetri Real-Time
- Stream data telemetri tegangan ($V$), arus ($A$), *Power Factor* ($\cos \phi$), dan frekuensi ($Hz$) dieksekusi secara sub-milidetik ($<0.01\text{ ms/op}$).

### C. Analisis Bisnis & Financial ROI Engine
